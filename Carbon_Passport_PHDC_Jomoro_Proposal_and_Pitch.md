# Part 2 — Executive Proposal

## 1. The Opportunity

The Jomoro Petroleum Hub will involve multiple, separately operated industrial assets with different engineering, control and information systems. If carbon data management is designed only after facilities are commissioned, PHDC and individual operators may face inconsistent calculation boundaries, fragmented supporting evidence, duplicated reporting work and slower reviews.

**Proposed response:** Establish a common **Carbon Passport MRV framework** during FEED and early project planning, allowing individual operators to manage their own data while PHDC receives agreed, permissioned, aggregated oversight.

### Strategic outcomes

| For PHDC | For BOO consortium operators | For financiers, buyers and reviewers |
|---|---|---|
| Common reporting definitions and visibility across participating facilities | Standardised activity-data intake and emissions calculations | Traceable calculation methods and evidence packages |
| Progress against defined emissions and mitigation indicators | Reduced manual reconciliation across teams | Easier access to review-ready ESG information |
| Permissioned consolidated dashboards | Product/batch carbon records where relevant | Clear provenance, version history and documented review status |

## 2. Proposed Carbon Passport Solution

Carbon Passport is proposed as a **digital carbon record and workflow layer**, not as a replacement for plant process control, regulator approvals or accredited third-party assurance.

### End-to-end workflow

```text
INDUSTRIAL ACTIVITY DATA
  SCADA / historian exports | electricity meters | fuel records
  flare logs | process data | logistics / material inputs
                         |
                         v
DATA INGESTION & QUALITY CHECKS
  API / file imports / manual records / SaurEdge pilot connectors
  Unit normalisation | completeness | source tagging
                         |
                         v
EMISSIONS & PRODUCT FOOTPRINT ENGINE
  Facility boundaries | Scope 1 / Scope 2 | applicable Scope 3
  Emission factors | assumptions | calculation versions
                         |
                         v
EVIDENCE, REVIEW & AUDIT TRAIL
  Meter readings | invoices | calibration documents
  Methodology references | role-based reviewer decisions
                         |
                         v
CARBON PASSPORT / REPORTING OUTPUTS
  Facility records | product / batch footprints
  Permissions-controlled dashboards | exportable audit packages
  Optional public QR verification for issued records
```

### Core functional modules

- **Operator onboarding:** facility, legal-entity, site and asset registration.
- **Activity-data capture:** manual upload, CSV/API integration and selected industrial meter connectivity evaluation through SaurEdge.
- **Emissions accounting:** documented boundaries, factors, formulas, assumptions and calculation results.
- **Evidence vault:** source files and references connected to particular calculations and versions.
- **Verification workflow:** company preparation, reviewer checks, evidence requests, recorded decisions and controlled status changes.
- **Carbon Passport records:** product-level or batch-level versions, history and optional public verification pages.
- **PHDC dashboard:** consolidated views of agreed data from participating operators, with access control.
- **Reporting exports:** configurable packages aligned with mutually agreed templates, subject to applicable authority specifications.

**Important status distinction:** A Carbon Passport platform review status is not a claim of independent accreditation or external regulatory certification. Third-party assurance requires an appropriately qualified/authorised reviewer and an agreed assurance scope.

## 3. Proposed Dashboard for PHDC

A permissioned PHDC-level dashboard could display:

1. Participating operator and facility onboarding progress.
2. Reporting completeness and evidence coverage.
3. Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions by facility and time period (where applicable and authorised).
4. Fuel, electricity, flaring and process activity indicators.
5. Carbon intensity trends for selected products and operational units.
6. Open evidence requests, review statuses and data-quality alerts.
7. Approved mitigation project indicators and estimated versus substantiated impacts.

**Data governance principle:** Operators retain their underlying records and rights. PHDC sees only the categories, aggregation levels and outputs expressly approved under the agreed participation and confidentiality model.

## 4. Reporting and Compliance Alignment

The pilot should first inventory the specific reporting duties of PHDC and participating facilities and confirm them with relevant local specialists. The platform can then configure **data structures and export templates** suited to those needs.

Potential reference frameworks include:

- Applicable Ghana Environmental Protection Authority requirements and national climate reporting needs.
- Relevant sector-level petroleum, energy and environmental reporting requirements, as confirmed by Ghanaian advisers and competent authorities.
- GHG Protocol principles for organisational greenhouse gas inventories and relevant product accounting guidance.
- IFC Performance Standards and Equator Principles information needs, where applicable to a specific financing transaction.
- EU CBAM for **covered goods** and import pathways only; other product carbon footprint standards or buyer requests for non-covered products.

**No blanket compliance claims:** The platform supports preparation and traceability. Reporting compliance, lender acceptance and regulatory approvals require assessment against the specific project, jurisdiction, product and reporting period.

## 5. Four-Month Pilot Roadmap

| Phase | Timing | Activities | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Architecture and reporting alignment** | Months 1–2 | PHDC technical workshop; select pilot use case; map source systems, emissions boundaries, reporting requirements, data-access rules and roles | Signed-off pilot scope, integration map, sample reporting schema and acceptance criteria |
| **B — Controlled pilot implementation** | Months 3–4 | Onboard one willing consortium/workstream; ingest representative records; run calculations; upload evidence; simulate reviewer workflow; build dashboard | Demonstration carbon records, data-quality report, sample audit export, PHDC dashboard demonstration and scale-up assessment |

### Recommended pilot boundary

Choose **one clearly defined facility, project package or operational data stream**, rather than promising full enclave coverage during the pilot. Where physical plants are not operating, use engineering specifications, representative or test datasets and label all outputs **provisional / demonstration only**.

### Suggested success criteria

- A documented chain from activity-data source to calculation and supporting evidence.
- Configured emission factors and calculation methodologies with version history.
- Successful role-based separation of operator, PHDC viewer and reviewer permissions.
- Working export of an audit-ready *data package* (not a claim of independently certified results).
- A list of actual integration, assurance, cost and governance requirements for the next phase.

## 6. Delivery, Governance and Commercial Discussion

**Proposed delivery model:** Saurient AI Labs Pvt. Ltd. supplies the Carbon Passport technology; any Ghanaian local delivery, engineering, environmental or assurance partner would be agreed before mobilisation. Local-content and data-hosting requirements should be reviewed by qualified Ghanaian advisers.

**Commercial approach:** No pricing or exclusivity is assumed in this introductory proposal. After scope validation, parties may discuss pilot cost, licensing, data integration, equipment, hosting, on-site support, ownership, confidentiality and expansion options in a separate commercial proposal.

**Requested next step:** PHDC nominates a technical/sustainability liaison and identifies an interested consortium or defined reporting use case for a joint discovery session.
